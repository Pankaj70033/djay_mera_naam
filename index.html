About

बिहार, India

कलाकार from बिहार

DJAY is a rapper and artist from Bihar, India.

He started his musical journey in 2021, beginning with romantic songs before expanding into music focused on political awareness and social issues. In the years since, his writing and his sound have moved toward real-world experience — giving voice to the issues and the perspectives around him.

His album Bihar Ki Pukar is out now, an important chapter in that journey. He is currently working on new projects, exploring the next direction of his sound and his storytelling. More details and updates on the upcoming work will be shared soon.
